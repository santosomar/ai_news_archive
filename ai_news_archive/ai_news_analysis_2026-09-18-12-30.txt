ADDITIONAL ANALYSIS:

### Summary of Papers and Articles Related to Using AI for Security or Securing AI

#### 1. **MAGS: Multi-agent Auto-formalization Guarantees Safety for Agentic Outputs**
   - **Summary**: This paper introduces MAGS, a framework that generates executable programs with formal safety guarantees. It uses Dafny as a verification-aware intermediate representation, ensuring that generated code adheres to specified safety properties. The framework is evaluated across various tasks, achieving a 100% success rate in producing programs with non-trivial safety guarantees.
   - **Security Insight**: MAGS addresses the challenge of ensuring safety in AI-generated code, which is crucial for preventing vulnerabilities in software systems.

#### 2. **Closed-World Resolution Against Tool Hallucination in LLM Agents**
   - **Summary**: This study identifies a significant blind spot in tool-augmented LLM agents, where they may call non-existent tools, leading to erroneous outputs. The paper proposes a taxonomy of tool hallucination and introduces a closed-world resolver to address this issue.
   - **Security Insight**: The findings highlight the importance of robust verification mechanisms to prevent LLMs from making unsafe or incorrect tool calls, which could lead to security vulnerabilities.

#### 3. **Characterizing Web Search by Conversational LLM Agents**
   - **Summary**: This research investigates how conversational LLM agents utilize web search and the quality of their decision-making processes. It finds that while web search invocation is common, it does not always lead to improved response quality.
   - **Security Insight**: The study raises concerns about the reliability of information sourced from web searches, emphasizing the need for secure and accurate information retrieval methods in AI systems.

#### 4. **Do AI Agents Understand Computer Architecture?**
   - **Summary**: This paper explores whether AI agents can effectively design hardware, distinguishing between genuine understanding and mere parameter tuning. It introduces AutoTuring, a framework for evaluating agent understanding of computer architecture.
   - **Security Insight**: Understanding the capabilities and limitations of AI in hardware design is essential for ensuring that AI-generated designs do not introduce security flaws.

#### 5. **When Hiring Becomes Agent-Mediated: Evaluating Access and Recurrence in Two-Agent Résumé Screening**
   - **Summary**: This study examines a two-agent system for résumé screening, comparing it to traditional methods. The findings suggest that agent-mediated screening can change the dynamics of hiring decisions.
   - **Security Insight**: The implications of AI in hiring processes raise concerns about bias and fairness, necessitating secure and transparent AI systems to ensure equitable outcomes.

#### 6. **When AI Agents Commit: Cognitive Serializability Across Data, Evidence, Policy, and Authority**
   - **Summary**: This paper discusses the challenges of ensuring that AI agents' decisions are consistent and traceable, especially when multiple inputs influence outcomes. It proposes a cognitive serializability framework to manage these complexities.
   - **Security Insight**: Ensuring that AI decisions are traceable and consistent is critical for accountability and security, particularly in high-stakes environments.

#### 7. **When Retrieval Metrics Mislead: Measuring Policy Signal in Long-Horizon Tool-Use Agents**
   - **Summary**: This research critiques the reliance on exact-match retrieval metrics for evaluating the utility of retrieved information in agent decision-making. It emphasizes the need for a more nuanced understanding of retrieval effectiveness.
   - **Security Insight**: The findings suggest that relying solely on retrieval metrics can lead to misjudgments in agent performance, which could have security implications if agents make decisions based on incomplete or misleading information.

#### 8. **When Self-Evolution Backfires: Pre-Commit Gating against Skill Contamination in LLM Agents**
   - **Summary**: This paper identifies the risks of skill contamination in self-evolving agents and proposes a framework for pre-commit gating to prevent the introduction of flawed skills.
   - **Security Insight**: The study underscores the importance of robust governance mechanisms in AI systems to prevent the propagation of errors and vulnerabilities through self-evolution.

#### 9. **When Summaries Distort Decisions: Information Fidelity in LLM-Compressed Financial Analysis**
   - **Summary**: This paper examines how LLM-generated summaries can mislead decision-making in financial contexts, emphasizing the need for fidelity in information representation.
   - **Security Insight**: The findings highlight the risks associated with relying on AI-generated summaries for critical decisions, necessitating secure methods for validating and verifying AI outputs.
