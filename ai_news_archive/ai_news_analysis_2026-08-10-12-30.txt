#### Notable Papers and Contributions
- **LoRAScan**: A framework for detecting and rejecting trigger-bearing inputs in backdoored adapters, achieving high accuracy in identifying malicious inputs while maintaining performance on clean inputs.
  
- **Krum-Proxy**: A selection-aware backdoor injection strategy that optimizes malicious updates to infiltrate the dense core of benign distributions, demonstrating the vulnerabilities of distance-based aggregation methods.

- **Trustworthy Agent Networks**: A conceptual framework emphasizing the need for trust to be built into the architecture of agent networks from the outset, rather than retrofitted.

- **Counterfactual Shapley Credit Assignment**: A novel framework for credit assignment in reinforcement learning that isolates causal drivers of observed outcomes from spurious correlations.

- **FinanceHarness**: A framework for automating financial deep research, demonstrating the effectiveness of specialized harness designs in improving research quality.

- **Multi-Legal-Bench**: A cross-jurisdictional legal benchmark that evaluates identical tasks across multiple countries and languages, providing insights into the performance of LLMs in legal reasoning.
